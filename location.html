Find us

Camp Sierra

Camp Sierra is located at 40801 CA-168, Big Creek, CA 93605 in the Sierra National Forest — about 60 miles northeast of Fresno.

💡 Tip: Download offline maps before you leave — cell service can be spotty in the mountains.

Explore the area

What's nearby

Shaver Lake

8 miles · 15 min

Boat rentals, swimming beaches, and several restaurants and bars in town. Great for a day trip or a morning on the water.

Huntington Lake

7 miles · 25 min

Swimming, sailing, and kayaking on a beautiful high-altitude reservoir. Less crowded than Shaver, with a couple of good spots for lunch.

Ken's Supermarket

Big Creek

Local grocery store with the legendary motto: "If we don't have it, you don't need it." Reportedly life-changing. Bring cash.

Sierra National Forest

All around you

Over 1.3 million acres of wilderness at your doorstep. Wildflowers in summer, stunning granite views, and trails for every fitness level.

Get outside

Favorite hikes & day trips

These are all within 30–45 minutes of camp. Noah will be leading at least one of these — attendance strongly encouraged.

  • Rancheria Falls
    2 miles round trip · Lush waterfall hike through mixed conifer forest. One of the most beautiful short hikes in the area.
    Easy
  • Indian Pools
    1.5 miles round trip · Natural granite swimming holes on the San Joaquin River. Perfect for a hot afternoon.
    Easy
  • Dinkey Lakes Wilderness
    Variable · Series of alpine lakes surrounded by granite. Endless options from short loops to full-day adventures.
    Moderate
  • Kaiser Peak
    10 miles round trip · Summit hike with panoramic Sierra views. Not for the faint of heart — or anyone who overindulged at the scotch tasting.
    Strenuous
  • Shaver Lake Shoreline
    Variable · Easy lakeside walking with views of the water and surrounding pines. Good for morning coffee walks.
    Easy
