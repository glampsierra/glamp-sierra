Pricing

Your total is lodging + meal plan. The registration form calculates it all for you automatically.

Step 1

Choose your lodging

Room costs are split evenly among everyone sharing. The more friends you bring, the cheaper it gets.

Most affordable
Dorm
$300 / room per weekend
Rooms in a shared lodge building. Each room has 2 bunks (4 beds). The lodge has shared bathrooms with sinks, toilets, and showers.


Note: bring your own sleeping bag or sheets, pillow, and towel.
Most popular
Duplex
$700 / room per weekend
Private entry, private bathroom, one double bed plus bunk beds (up to 6). Includes microwave and mini-fridge.


Sheets and towels provided.
Premium
King's Cabin
$850 / cabin per weekend
A full private cabin with living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, and private deck. Sleeps up to 4.


Sheets and towels provided.
Step 2

Choose your meal plan

Each person chooses their own plan. Both include all meals for the full weekend.

🍷
Full plan
$250 / person
  • All meals (Thu dinner through Sun breakfast)
  • Wine & cheese nightly
  • Full open bar
  • Featured tasting
  • All non-alcoholic beverages
🥤
No-alcohol plan
$175 / person
  • All meals (Thu dinner through Sun breakfast)
  • All non-alcoholic beverages
  • Coffee, tea, juices, sodas
  • Everything except the alcohol
Your total = lodging share + meal plan. For example: 4 friends sharing a Duplex ($700 ÷ 4 = $175 each) on the full plan ($250) = $425/person for the whole weekend. The registration form calculates this automatically as you fill it out — no math required.
